Refeeding / catch-up calculator

Catch-up caloric needs are estimated from the classic formula (RDA × IBW ÷ actual weight). IBW is the 50th-percentile weight for the child's length/height from the selected growth standard. Calculate Z-scores first.

How to use

  1. Pick the Adult or Pediatric tab.
  2. Enter anthropometrics, days of poor intake, and baseline + current electrolytes. Optional fields refine the result.
  3. Press Calculate. You get a risk verdict, caloric-needs & energy-deficit estimate, a degree-of-starvation summary, and (peds) an AND/ASPEN malnutrition grade.
  4. Every computed number has a “show calculation” toggle with the formula and substituted values, plus a link to the source.
  5. Copy the ASCII Assessment & Plan block straight into the chart.

What it computes

  • Refeeding risk — ASPEN 2020 adult (Table 3) & pediatric (Table 5) criteria; NICE CG32 (adult); electrolyte-shift severity (mild/moderate/severe by % drop within 5 days).
  • Pediatric malnutrition — AND/ASPEN 2014 single-point z-scores and multi-point trend indicators.
  • Caloric needs — Schofield (weight-only, peds) or Mifflin–St Jeor (adults), × activity/stress factor.
  • Energy deficit / degree of starvation — % of needs met, daily & cumulative deficit, WHO BMI thinness band.
